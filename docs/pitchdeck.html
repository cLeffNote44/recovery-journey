The Challenge

Substance abuse treatment facilities face four systemic problems that undermine patient outcomes and operational efficiency.

!

Reactive, Not Predictive

Most facilities only learn about relapse risk after it happens. Staff respond to crises instead of preventing them. By the time warning signs are noticed, the window for early intervention has closed.

⇄

Disconnected Systems

Patient data lives in paper forms, spreadsheets, separate EHRs, and text messages. Nothing talks to each other. Staff waste hours re-entering data and hunting for information across fragmented tools.

◌

Patient Disengagement

Between sessions, patients have no structured way to track progress, practice skills, or communicate with their care team. The critical hours between appointments are unsupported.

◆

Compliance Burden

HIPAA and 42 CFR Part 2 compliance requires extensive documentation, audit trails, and secure communication. Most facilities handle this manually, increasing cost and error risk.

What Sets Us Apart

Six features that no other recovery platform offers.

◔ Traffic Light System

Patients build their own Green/Yellow/Red zone action plans. Green for daily maintenance, Yellow for warning signs, Red for crisis response — with quick-dial to sponsors and therapists.

✎ 12-Step Digital Work

Full step-by-step progress tracking with reflections, exercises, and sponsor notes. The first platform to digitize the 12-step process.

★ Money Saved Calculator

Show patients the tangible financial benefit of sobriety. Configurable cost-per-day tracks cumulative savings — real motivation beyond milestones.

⚠ 4 Crisis Protocols

Not just hotline numbers. Four structured protocols: Immediate Urge, High-Risk Situation, Emotional Crisis, and Trigger Management — each with timed steps and grounding exercises.

▢ Smart Form-Fill

14 templates that convert paper underscores into proper digital fields. Checkboxes become real checkboxes. Dates become date inputs. No more broken formatting.

◉ Offline-First Privacy

Patient data stays on their device by default. No cloud dependency. No data collection. Optional sync when they choose. HIPAA-compliant by architecture, not just policy.

Unlike platforms that serve all behavioral health broadly, RecoverJourney is built specifically for substance abuse treatment.

Built for Compliance from Day One

Not bolted on after the fact

HIPAA Privacy & Security Rule

Full administrative, physical, and technical safeguards. Access controls, audit trails, transmission security, and data integrity — all built into the platform architecture.

42 CFR Part 2

Substance use disorder confidentiality requirements met at every level. Consent tracking, re-disclosure prohibition, and separate access controls for SUD-specific data.

Data Encryption

AES-256 encryption at rest. TLS 1.3 for all data in transit. WebSocket connections encrypted end-to-end. Certificate pinning for mobile applications.

Audit Logging

Every PHI access logged with user, timestamp, action, resource, and fields viewed. Immutable records with batch-queued logging and immediate dispatch for critical events.

Additional Security Features

✓ 15-min session timeout with 2-min warning

✓ Tokens stored in memory only

✓ Two-factor authentication

✓ Role-based access control (3 tiers)

✓ Input sanitization via DOMPurify

✓ BAA available for covered entities
